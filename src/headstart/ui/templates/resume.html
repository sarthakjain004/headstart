{# The Résumé tab (ADR-0123, ADR-0124). The editor is entirely client-side and the browser is the working copy: every keystroke is written to this browser's own storage and to nothing else. There is now one way a résumé can leave it, and it is a switch the Account throws for one résumé at a time — "Keep a copy on my account", below, which stores that document in the private dataset the rest of an Account's records live in. Off by default, per résumé, and nothing else on this tab talks to the server. See resume_sync.js, ADR-0124 and ADR-0041 (whose rule for the pasted-in **Résumé** — read once, discarded — is untouched by any of this). The skeleton is static so the panel is legible before the scripts run; resume_editor.js fills the panes. TWO SEGMENTS — Edit and Preview (ADR-0128), narrowing ADR-0125's side-by-side arrangement. Writing a résumé and looking at one are two different sittings, and giving each the whole panel is what buys the form room to be a form: a job's six fields sat one-per-row in a 400px rail, and its bullets were paragraphs in a three-line box 330px wide. The split is ONE level of tabs, not a third. Design moves to Preview, where its effect is what you are looking at; Checks and Keywords move into a column beside the form, where the block they name is what you fix. So the four panes that were a segment plus a nested tablist — Document / Design / Checks / Keywords — are now two workspaces with no nesting at all. #}
{# ONE band, three groups, hairline-separated: the document, the version, the actions. Grouping is the whole point — the eight controls that used to sit here at one visual weight gave a newcomer no way to tell "Download" from "+ Version". Open / New / Import collapse into the Résumés menu, and layout, paper size and zoom are gone from the chrome entirely: they belong to the document and the preview, not to the session. It sits ABOVE both segments on purpose. Download is the highest-intent control on the tab and the last one anybody uses; a segment switch that hid it would be the one failure the research names outright — a control that is hardest to reach exactly when it is most wanted. #}
{# Tailoring: one résumé, many versions. A version stores only what it changes, so fixing a sentence in the master reaches every version that never disagreed with it. The button says what it is FOR — "+ Version" named the mechanism, and a newcomer reading it could not tell what would happen. #}

{# Keywords is HERE, not beside the form, and that is a measurement fact rather than a taste. Its answer is "is this term on the page, and is it in the top half of page one" — it reads the rendered sheet's own geometry, and a hidden element measures zero. Measured on a three-page résumé: a block 18.91in down the document reports 0in from the top while the paper is off screen, so every term would be scored "near the top" whatever the truth was. The highlight it draws is on that same sheet, so this is also simply where its output is. Folded away because it is a task somebody opts into once per application. #}
