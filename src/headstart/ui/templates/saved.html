Saved jobs

Your copy of each job is kept here even after it closes, so this list never loses one. A closed tag means the job is no longer in our index — almost always because the employer took it down, occasionally because we dropped the whole board (a board that goes dead, or one recorded twice under different spellings, is swept out wholesale and takes its still-open jobs with it). The link still goes to the employer, who is the one authority on whether it is open.
