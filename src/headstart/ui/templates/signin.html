Why the jobs hold up

  • Every link opens on the employer's own board. HeadStart never hosts an application, and there is no page in between the result and the company.
  • Closed roles get removed, and the exception is published. A job missing from its board twice running comes out of the index — but a board we cannot read properly is skipped rather than emptied, so some dead entries do persist — one such board was measured serving closed jobs for 22 days. The Data tab inside shows the mechanism.
  • The gaps are published too. Inside, a Data tab reports what share of jobs carry a salary, a posting date or an experience level — counted off the live index each time, not written down once.
  • Nothing here is paid placement. Results are ordered by similarity to what you asked for. No employer can buy a position, because there is nothing to buy.

Sign in to search

Open HeadStart to sign in ↗

Signing in stores your email address — that is the whole of what sign-in itself keeps. After that, only what you save on purpose: searches, starred jobs, a profile if you make one. Nothing is posted anywhere, no email is sent unless you switch one on yourself, and signing out drops the session. The Data tab lists all of it.
