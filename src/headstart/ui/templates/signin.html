Why the jobs hold up

  • Every link opens on the employer's own site. HeadStart never hosts an application, and there is no page in between the result and the company. Some sites belong to staffing firms or recruiters.
  • Closed roles get removed, and the exception is published. A job comes off once it is missing from the company's site twice in a row. When we can't read a site properly we leave its jobs up rather than wipe them, so some closed ones linger — one site kept showing closed jobs for 22 days.
  • Nothing here is paid placement. Results are ordered by similarity to what you asked for. No employer can buy a position, because there is nothing to buy.
