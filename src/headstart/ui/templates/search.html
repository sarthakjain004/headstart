Where

Matches every way a job names the country: its name, its states or provinces, its cities and its codes, so "Austin, TX" is in the United States and "München" is in Germany.
Picking an Indian city also matches its neighbourhoods and other spellings. Location below matches any place in the world, just without that. Jobs from every country are included either way.
