      How to read this

      Count is the number of jobs. It also rises when HeadStart adds companies, not only when they hire.

      Share is each line’s slice of the whole (all jobs, or the companies you picked), so adding companies doesn’t lift every line at once.

      Change starts every line at 100, so 120 means 20% more openings. The dashed line is HeadStart’s tracked stock, or the companies you picked: a line above it is growing faster than the whole.

      Grey vertical lines mark known counting changes. Recorded index additions and recorded closures/removals can be too low: some sites don’t show when a posting disappears, and days when HeadStart changed how it counts are left out. Recorded closures/removals are not confirmed employer closures: Dormant policy evictions and rule changes can affect them. Adjusted trends do not isolate causal hiring activity.

      {# A category's line only (ADR-0057): a job can change category, never company or title. #}

      A job can move to another category when HeadStart learns more about it, which looks like one job closing and another opening. Read a category’s trend over weeks, not one day’s jump.
