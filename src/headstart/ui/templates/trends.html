    How to read this

    A family's line moving is not the same as jobs opening or closing. Each job's family is worked out from the meaning of its text, and that text can be filled in later — so a job can move between families without changing at all, which looks exactly like one closing here and another opening there. One investigated decline turned out to be mostly that. This chart does not separate the two, so read the shape over weeks rather than a single step. Watch-listed roles are matched by title instead and are not affected. ADR-0057
