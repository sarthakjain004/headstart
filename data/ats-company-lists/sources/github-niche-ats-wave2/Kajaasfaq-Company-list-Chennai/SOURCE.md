## What it is
Community-driven markdown directory of 170+ companies with offices in Chennai, India. Each row =
company name, industry, **careers-page URL**, LinkedIn, email. Many careers URLs point directly at
an ATS host, so real per-company ATS slugs are extractable.

## Approx counts (this source)
- 170+ companies total (most careers links are own-domain, not ATS).
- **22 niche-provider ATS career URLs** extracted (the HeadStart-target slugs).
