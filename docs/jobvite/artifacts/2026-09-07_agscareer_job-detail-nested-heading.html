Account Executive- Slots

Canada


Date Posted: 09/23/2025

Apply Apply Later

Job Description

**Must live in Western Canada, (British Columbia or Alberta)
Job Summary
AGS is seeking a motivated Account Executive to drive sales of our slot and gaming products in a defined territory. This role develops and executes sales strategies, builds strong client relationships, and ensures outstanding customer service while achieving revenue targets.
 
Responsibilities
  • Build and manage relationships with casino operators and key decision-makers.
  • Develop and implement territory sales plans aligned with revenue goals.
  • Identify customer needs and deliver consultative product demonstrations.
  • Stay informed on industry trends and competitor activity.
  • Negotiate and prepare sales contracts and accurate forecasts.
  • Represent AGS at trade shows and industry events.
 
Qualifications
  • Bachelor’s degree in business/marketing preferred or equivalent experience.
  • 5+ years of sales experience; 2 years of gaming industry experience strongly preferred.
  • Proven record of meeting or exceeding sales quotas.
  • Strong communication, negotiation, and presentation skills.
  • Proficiency in MS Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook).
Note: All offers are contingent upon successful completion of a background check
 
*Posted positions are not open to third party recruiters and unsolicited resume submissions will be considered free referrals. 
 
AGS is an equal opportunity employer.

About Us

AGS is a global company focused on creating a diverse mix of entertaining gaming experiences for every kind of player.
